\section*{\color{principleblue} Section II \quad Leading the Team \quad \normalfont\small\color{midgray}(9 Principles)}
\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, label=\textbf{II.\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Demo Discipline} ---
        Demo what you have, on cadence. Positive discipline, not punishment.
  \item \textbf{Servant Leadership} ---
        The leader's job is to serve the team's ability to do the work.
  \item \textbf{Maintain Fundamentals Under Pressure} ---
        Good habits erode first under stress. Defend them hardest then.
  \item \textbf{The Power of Saying No} ---
        Protecting team focus is a leadership act.
  \item \textbf{Communication Infrastructure} ---
        Design your information flows deliberately.
  \item \textbf{Psychological Safety} ---
        Teams that can fail safely learn faster.
  \item \textbf{Humble / Parenting-Style Leadership} ---
        \tbd[Framing TBD---standalone principle or emotional underpinning
        of servant leadership (parenting analogy)? Resolve with Eric.]
  \item \textbf{Earn Your Real Estate} ---
        Internal promotion is a leadership responsibility, not an HR function.
  \item \textbf{Information Gatekeeping Is Toxicity} ---
        Hoarding information is invisible hierarchy. It destroys trust.
        \textit{(Story banked --- Section II confirmed, parent principle TBD.)}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{\color{principleblue} Section III \quad Perspective \& Framing \quad \normalfont\small\color{midgray}(10 Principles)}
\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em, label=\textbf{III.\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Engineers as Self-Managing} ---
        When engineers understand the full trade space, they self-direct effectively.
  \item \textbf{Vision Sourcing vs.\ Vision Sinking} ---
        Leaders source vision upward. They do not sink it.
  \item \textbf{Radical Optimism} ---
        Optimism is a discipline, not a mood.
  \item \textbf{Protect the Passion} ---
        Guard what drives people. Passion is the engine of frontier work.
  \item \textbf{Program Management from Day One} ---
        Time, cost, scope, and manufacturability are dimensions from day one.
  \item \textbf{Feedback Is the Multiplier} ---
        The quantity of feedback loops is a primary driver of outcomes.
  \item \textbf{The Fulcrum Principle} ---
        Physical proximity to your team is a leadership act.
